What Sweet Bonanza is

How Sweet Bonanza pays. Sweet Bonanza is an online slot, not a table game and not a crash game. It uses a scatter pays model: symbols do not need to land on a payline, they pay when enough matching symbols appear anywhere on the grid at the same time. Winning symbols are then removed and new symbols drop in to fill the gaps, which is the tumble feature. Tumbles keep repeating for as long as each drop creates a new win, so a single paid spin can resolve into a chain of wins. The lollipop scatter symbol is what triggers the free spins round, and multiplier symbols land during that round and combine with the tumble chain. The figures below come from the official Pragmatic Play game page. Landing 8 to 12 or more matching symbols anywhere on the grid awards up to 50x your stake. Four to six lollipop scatters award up to 100x and trigger the bonus game with 10 free spins. Multiplier symbols worth up to 100x can land during that feature. The grid is 6x5. How does Sweet Bonanza work?
