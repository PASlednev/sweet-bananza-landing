Sweet Bonanza RTP: One Game, Several Settings

The headline figure is 96.51%. It is not the only one Pragmatic Play ships, and the version your operator has enabled is printed inside the game — not on the review site that sent you there.

By Ryan Murton · Crash games analyst 21 August 2026 Last updated 28 August 2026

🔞 18+ only · Real-money gambling

Almost every page about this game quotes one number: 96.51%. It is the default configuration and, for most licensed operators, it is the correct one. But Pragmatic Play ships Sweet Bonanza with more than one RTP setting, and the choice belongs to the casino, not to you.

Why one game has several figures

Studios supply a slot as a configurable product. The maths model is fixed; the return is offered in tiers, and the operator picks one when the game is integrated. Two casinos can run the same title, the same artwork and the same paytable, with a materially different long-run return.

Treat the exact decimals in any article — including this one — as indicative. The figure that governs your session is the one displayed inside the client you are actually playing.

How to check it in thirty seconds

  1. Open the game at the casino you intend to play at, in real-money mode.

  2. Open the menu, then the information or paytable screen.

  3. Scroll to the last page. The return to player is stated there, along with the separate figures for Ante Bet and the feature buy if the operator offers them.

  4. If the panel shows a range rather than a single figure, the lower end is what applies when the optional bets are in play.

A one-point difference in RTP is invisible over an evening and expensive over a year. It costs nothing to look, and the panel cannot be wrong — it is generated by the same build you are playing.

Volatility matters more than the last decimal

Sweet Bonanza is a high-volatility slot with a payout ceiling in the tens of thousands of times the stake. That ceiling is funded by long stretches of nothing. The practical consequence is that the RTP figure describes an average you will not personally experience over a normal number of spins — your session is a small, noisy sample of it.

Which is why stake sizing does more work than casino shopping: a stake that survives two hundred flat spins keeps you in the game long enough for the free spins round to matter. The full numbers are in the RTP and max win breakdown, and the licensing side is covered in is Sweet Bonanza legit.
