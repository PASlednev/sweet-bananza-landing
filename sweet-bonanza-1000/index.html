Sweet Bonanza 1000 is a separate Pragmatic Play release, not a re-skin of the original. It keeps the scatter pays grid and the tumble feature but raises the multiplier ceiling, and it publishes its own RTP, volatility rating and maximum win. This page sets the two side by side so you can see which figures actually change.

What stayed the same

The core loop is unchanged. Matching candy and fruit symbols pay wherever they land on the 6x5 grid, winning symbols are removed, replacements tumble in, and the chain continues while each drop produces a win. The lollipop scatter still triggers the free spins round, and multiplier symbols still land inside it. If you know the original, you do not need to relearn the game. How Sweet Bonanza works, full slot guide covers the shared mechanics in full.

What changed

The headline change is the multiplier ceiling, which is what the 1000 in the name points at. Pragmatic Play describes the release as a supercharged sequel offering wins of up to 25,000x, against the original's multiplier symbols topping out at 100x. In the 1000 bonus game those symbols become candy bombs awarding random values of up to 1,000x. A higher top multiplier lifts the maximum win and typically sharpens volatility, meaning rarer but larger outcomes.

The bonus round is also more generous on retriggers: at least four lollipops unlock it with 10 free spins, and five further free spins are added whenever three or more lollipops land during the bonus.

Which one suits which player

A higher multiplier ceiling is not an upgrade for everyone. It concentrates more of the theoretical return into rarer outcomes, so the same bankroll typically produces longer quiet stretches. If you play short sessions or dislike long droughts, the original profile may suit you better. If you accept a harsher distribution for a higher ceiling, the 1000 release is the one built for that. Neither choice changes the house edge in your favour, and neither rewards a stake pattern or a spin timing rule.
